✕ Destination changed after you inspected it

Same code. New destination.

You didn't rescan anything. You didn't approve anything. The speaker flipped a server-side toggle and the identical sticker now sends you somewhere else.

Nothing happened to your device. On a real attack this is where a payment page, a fake login, or a malware prompt would sit — served to everyone who scanned a code that was benign when they checked it. That's why "I inspected the URL once" is not a defense for a QR you don't control.
