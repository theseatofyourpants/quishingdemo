✕ You landed on the lookalike domain

Your eyes read “bank.” The QR sent you to “bаnk.”

One character was a Cyrillic “а” instead of a Latin “a.” Spottable when typed. Invisible inside a QR — there was nothing to read.

Nothing happened. This page is on the speaker's own domain. On a real attack this would be a pixel-perfect login clone on a near-identical address you never got the chance to inspect.
